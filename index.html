01 Distrito · in production

Hospital agents over WhatsApp

A large São Paulo hospital needed routine services on WhatsApp. The dominant challenge isn't conversation: each service touches a different internal system with its own rules, and the patient doesn't know — and shouldn't have to know — which service they are asking for.

 01 Distrito · em produção

Agentes hospitalares no WhatsApp

Um hospital de grande porte em São Paulo precisava oferecer serviços de rotina por WhatsApp. O desafio dominante não é conversar: cada serviço toca um sistema interno diferente, com regra própria, e o paciente não sabe — nem deveria saber — qual serviço está pedindo.

 02 Distrito · in development

Academic coaching agent

For an international education network: a conversational coach over a tool service that exposes institutional data by REST and MCP — the same access logic behind both, so other client systems consume it without going through the agent.

 02 Distrito · em desenvolvimento

Agente de coaching acadêmico

Para uma rede de ensino internacional: um coach conversacional sobre um serviço de tools que expõe dado institucional por REST e MCP — a mesma lógica de acesso atrás das duas, então outros sistemas do cliente consomem sem passar pelo agente.

 03 Distrito · daily use

Synthetic jury

A campaign research platform: clustering the audience base to find profiles that actually exist in the data, personas anchored to those clusters rather than invented, and simulated interviews against advertising stimuli.

 03 Distrito · uso diário

 04 PhD · published

ML-accelerated microscopy

Sparse sampling plus Gaussian-process regression to reconstruct s-SNOM images from a fraction of the measurements — published in Nano Letters 24(7), 2149 (2024).
